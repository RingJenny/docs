Ring Jenny handles the full booking process before the caller even hangs up. Instead of asking callers to click a link or wait for a confirmation text, Ring Jenny checks your calendar, offers available time slots, and locks in the appointment — all in a single, natural conversation.

<Note>
  Appointment booking is available on the **Scale ($149/mo)** and **Growth ($299/mo)** plans. Upgrade anytime from Dashboard > Billing.
</Note>

## How It Works

When a caller asks to schedule an appointment, Ring Jenny takes over the entire booking flow automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Caller requests an appointment">
    The caller says something like "I'd like to book a consultation" or "Can I schedule a visit?" Ring Jenny recognizes the intent instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ring Jenny checks your calendar">
    Ring Jenny queries your connected Lunacal calendar in real time and retrieves your next available openings based on your availability hours and buffer settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ring Jenny offers time slots">
    Ring Jenny reads out 2–3 available options to the caller and lets them choose the time that works best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Booking is confirmed">
    Once the caller picks a slot, Ring Jenny confirms the appointment out loud and creates the calendar entry immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="SMS confirmation is sent to the caller">
    Before the call ends, the caller automatically receives a text message with the date, time, and any instructions you've configured (e.g., parking info, what to bring).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set Up Appointment Booking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your Lunacal calendar">
    Go to **Dashboard > Integrations > Calendar** and connect your Lunacal account. Lunacal AI Scheduling is included free with all Ring Jenny plans — no separate subscription required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your availability and buffer time">
    Inside Lunacal, define your working hours and add buffer time between appointments. Buffer time prevents Ring Jenny from scheduling back-to-back bookings without a break.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Appointment Booking on your agent">
    Navigate to **Dashboard > Settings > Agent** and toggle on **Appointment Booking**. This tells Ring Jenny to actively offer scheduling during calls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with a live call">
    Call your Ring Jenny number and say you'd like to book an appointment. Verify that Ring Jenny offers real slots, confirms the booking, and sends the SMS confirmation to your test number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Always set buffer time between appointments in Lunacal. Without it, Ring Jenny may schedule consecutive bookings with no gap — leaving no time to prepare between clients.
</Tip>

## SMS Confirmation for Callers

After every successful booking, the caller automatically receives an SMS confirmation containing:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Appointment Details" icon="calendar">
    The confirmed date and time of the appointment, formatted clearly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your Custom Instructions" icon="note-sticky">
    Any notes you've added — location directions, what to prepare, cancellation policy, etc.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reschedule & Cancel Link" icon="arrow-rotate-left">
    A link the caller can tap to manage their appointment without calling back.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your Business Name" icon="building">
    The confirmation is sent under your business identity so callers recognize it immediately.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Reschedules and Cancellations

Callers who need to change or cancel their appointment can tap the link included in their confirmation text. The link takes them to your Lunacal booking page where they can self-manage their appointment — no call required, no manual work for you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="What if my calendar has no available slots?">
  Ring Jenny will let the caller know that no openings are currently available and offer to take a message or suggest they call back. You can also configure Ring Jenny to offer a callback option in your agent settings.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I customize the SMS confirmation message?">
  Yes. Go to **Dashboard > Settings > Appointment Booking** to edit the confirmation message template. Add your address, special instructions, or any other details you want every caller to receive.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Lunacal cost extra?">
  No. Lunacal AI Scheduling is included at no additional cost with all Ring Jenny plans, including the base Starter plan. Appointment booking activation, however, requires Scale or Growth.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if the caller doesn't have a phone number that can receive SMS?">
  Ring Jenny will still confirm the booking verbally on the call. The SMS confirmation is sent when possible; if delivery fails, the appointment is still created on your calendar.
</Accordion>
