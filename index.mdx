## How Ring Jenny Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your business info">
    Paste your website URL or Google Business Profile link. Ring Jenny reads your hours, services, and key details automatically — no manual data entry needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize your agent">
    Set your greeting, add custom FAQs, configure spam filtering, and give Ring Jenny a test call to verify her responses before going live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Forward your calls">
    A 30-second change to your phone settings routes incoming calls to Ring Jenny. Your customers hear a seamless, professional experience from the first ring.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stay in the loop">
    After every call, you receive an instant SMS and email notification with a full summary, transcript, and any action taken — so you always know what happened.
  </Step>
</Steps>
