---
title: "How do I update my card on file?"
sidebarTitle: "Billing"
description: "Need to update your payment method for your Ring Jenny account? No problem, just follow the steps below:"
mode: "wide"
---

1. Log into your Ring Jenny account
2. Navigate to the Account tab, then the Billing tab
3. Select "Billing' from the Payment Details & Invoices section. This will open the Stripe billing portal
4. Select 'Add payment method'
5. Ensure 'Use as default payment method' box is checked
6. Click 'Add'
