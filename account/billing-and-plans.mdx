Ring Jenny offers three plans built around the volume of calls your business handles each month. You can switch plans, change your billing cycle, or cancel at any time — all from your billing settings. This page covers everything you need to manage your subscription with confidence.

<Note>
  Your 7-day free trial automatically converts to your chosen paid plan when the trial ends. If you don't want to be charged, cancel before the trial period is up at **Dashboard > Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription**.
</Note>

## Upgrading Your Plan

Move to a higher plan whenever your call volume grows — the upgrade takes effect immediately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing Settings">
    Go to **Dashboard > Settings > Billing** and click **Change Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your New Plan">
    Choose the plan you'd like to upgrade to. You'll see a summary of what's included and the new monthly or annual price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Upgrade">
    Review the details and click **Confirm**. Your new plan and increased minute allowance activate immediately. You'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your current billing period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Downgrading Your Plan

You can downgrade to a lower plan at any time. The change takes effect at the end of your current billing period so you don't lose access to features you've already paid for.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing Settings">
    Go to **Dashboard > Settings > Billing** and click **Change Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Lower Plan">
    Choose the plan you'd like to move to and review what will change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Downgrade">
    Click **Confirm**. Your current plan remains active until the end of the billing period, then switches automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  When you downgrade, any features that are not included in your new plan — such as appointment booking, call transfers, warm transfers, and text messaging — will **stop working at the end of your current billing period**. Make sure your team is aware of the change before it takes effect.
</Warning>

## Cancelling Your Subscription

Cancel your subscription at any time. You'll keep full access to Ring Jenny until the end of your current billing period.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Billing Settings">
    Navigate to **Dashboard > Settings > Billing** and click **Cancel Subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Cancellation">
    Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm. You'll receive a cancellation confirmation email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Prefer to cancel by email? Reach out to [support@ringjenny.com](mailto:support@ringjenny.com) and the team will take care of it for you.

## Billing Date and Payment Methods

<Accordion title="When will I be charged?">
  You're billed on the same calendar day each month from the date you first subscribed. For example, if you started your paid plan on the 12th, you'll be charged on the 12th of every month.

  For annual plans, you're billed once per year on the anniversary of your subscription start date.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
  Ring Jenny accepts all major credit and debit cards, including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. You can update your payment method at any time from **Dashboard > Settings > Billing > Payment Method**.
</Accordion>

## Downloading Invoices

Access and download PDF receipts for any past payment directly from your dashboard.

1. Go to **Dashboard > Settings > Billing > Invoices**.
2. Find the payment you need a receipt for.
3. Click **Download PDF** next to that invoice.

Invoices are available immediately after each charge and include an itemized breakdown of your plan and billing period.
