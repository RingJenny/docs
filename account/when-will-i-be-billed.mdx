---
title: "When will I be billed?"
mode: "wide"
---

**Initial Charge:**

- Your first charge will occur at the end of your trial period. Your free trial period lasts 7 days.

**Monthly Billing Cycle:**

- After your initial charge, you will be automatically billed on a monthly basis. Your billing date will be the same day of the month as your first charge.

**Example:**

- If you transition to a paid plan on August 8th, your next payment will be on September 8th.
