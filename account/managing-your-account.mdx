Your Ring Jenny account settings give you full control over your profile, security, team access, and notifications — all from one place. This page walks you through the most common account management tasks so you can keep everything up to date.

<Tip>
  Keep your email address current. Ring Jenny sends all account notifications, usage alerts, and billing receipts to the email on file.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Updating your business name or contact details in your profile does **not** automatically re-train Ring Jenny. After making profile changes, go to **Dashboard \> Training** to update her knowledge about your business.
</Note>

## Account Settings

<Accordion title="Updating your profile">
  Keep your name, business name, email address, and phone number accurate so Ring Jenny and your team always have the right information.

  1. Go to **Dashboard \> Settings \> Profile**.
  2. Edit any of the following fields:
     - **Full name** — your personal name associated with the account
     - **Business name** — the name Ring Jenny uses when answering calls
     - **Email address** — used for notifications, receipts, and login
     - **Phone number** — your primary contact number
  3. Click **Save Changes**.

  Your updates take effect immediately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Changing your password">
  Reset your password at any time from the Security settings page.

  1. Go to **Dashboard \> Settings \> Security**.
  2. Click **Change Password**.
  3. Enter your **current password**, then enter and confirm your **new password**.
  4. Click **Update Password**.

  You'll receive a confirmation email once your password has been changed. If you didn't request the change, contact support immediately at [support@ringjenny.com](mailto:support@ringjenny.com).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Adding team members">
  Give your staff access to Ring Jenny by inviting them to your account and assigning the right role for each person.

  1. Go to **Dashboard \> Settings \> Team**.
  2. Click **Invite Member**.
  3. Enter the team member's **email address** and select their **role**:
     - **Admin** — full access to settings, billing, and training
     - **Member** — can view call logs and insights, but cannot change settings or billing
  4. Click **Send Invitation**.

  The invited person will receive an email with a link to create their account. They must accept the invitation before they can access your Ring Jenny dashboard.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Notification preferences">
  Control which email notifications Ring Jenny sends you so your inbox stays relevant.

  1. Go to **Dashboard \> Settings \> Notifications**.
  2. Toggle notifications on or off for each category:
     - **New call summaries** — receive an email after each call with a transcript and summary
     - **Low minutes alerts** — get notified when you're approaching your monthly minute limit
     - **Billing and receipts** — invoices and payment confirmations
     - **Product updates** — news about new features and improvements
  3. Click **Save Preferences**.

  Changes take effect immediately for all future notifications.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Deleting your account">
  If you need to permanently remove your Ring Jenny account, you can do so from the Account settings page.

  1. Go to **Dashboard \> Settings \> Account**.
  2. Scroll to the **Danger Zone** section.
  3. Click **Delete Account**.
  4. Confirm by typing your account email address when prompted, then click **Confirm Delete**.

  <Warning>
    Account deletion is **permanent and irreversible**. All your data — including call logs, transcripts, training information, and team members — will be immediately and permanently deleted. There is no way to recover your account after this action. If you'd like help before deleting, email us at [support@ringjenny.com](mailto:support@ringjenny.com).
  </Warning>
</Accordion>
