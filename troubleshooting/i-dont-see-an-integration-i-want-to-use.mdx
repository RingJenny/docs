The Ring Jenny team is always building new and exciting technology, including new integrations! We put our customer needs first and foremost in our roadmap so hearing user needs is key to driving our product forward.

If you don't see an integration you need for your business to use Ring Jenny successfully, reach out to the Ring Jenny team \[<u>support@ringjenny.com</u>\] and let us know. It might be closer on our roadmap than you think, or we may be able to provide you with a temporary workaround until we build your requested integration software.
