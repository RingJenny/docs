Ring Jenny offers three plans designed to match businesses at different stages of growth. Whether you're a solo operator fielding a handful of calls per day or a busy team handling hundreds, there's a plan sized for your needs. Every plan includes a **7-day free trial with no credit card required** and free **Lunacal AI Scheduling** integration.

## What Are "Minutes"?

Minutes refer to the total amount of AI call time Ring Jenny uses on your behalf each month. Every second Ring Jenny spends on a call — greeting a customer, answering a question, booking an appointment, or taking a message — counts toward your monthly minute balance. Short spam calls that Ring Jenny screens and ends quickly use very few minutes. You can monitor your remaining minutes at any time from your dashboard.

<Note>
  To view your real-time minute usage and see a breakdown by call, visit [Usage & Minutes](/account/usage-and-minutes).
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change plans after I sign up?">
    Yes — you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from **Account  → Billing → Manage on Stripe**. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle so you keep your current features through the period you've already paid for.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I use all my minutes?">
    If you reach your monthly minute limit, Ring Jenny will continue to answer calls but will notify you that you've hit your limit. You can upgrade your plan mid-cycle to restore full service, or wait until your next billing cycle when your minutes reset. You'll receive a dashboard alert and an email notification when you reach 80% and 100% of your limit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the free trial include all plan features?">
    Yes. Your 7-day free trial gives you full access to every feature on the plan tier you select during signup. No features are locked or restricted during the trial period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is Lunacal AI Scheduling and why is it free?">
    Lunacal AI Scheduling is an AI-powered calendar and booking system that Ring Jenny uses to book, confirm, and reschedule appointments directly during calls. It's included at no extra cost on every Ring Jenny plan because appointment booking is a core part of what Ring Jenny does for your business.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a setup fee or contract?">
    No setup fees and no contracts. You pay only your plan's monthly or annual subscription rate. Cancel anytime from your account settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
