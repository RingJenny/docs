title: "How to set up email and text notifications"
description: "Too many accounts to manage already? We get it. You can set up email and/or text notifications to alert you any time you get a new call to your Ring Jenny account, giving you a brief overview of the call and a direct link to the call detail view."
mode: "wide"
---

To set up email and text notifications, log into your Ring Jenny account and navigate to the **Account** tab. Under the **Notifications** section toggle on the notifications you would like to receive.

If you would like to receive text notifications, you will need to designate a phone number capable of receiving SMS messages and all standard rates will apply.
