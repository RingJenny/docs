Call forwarding is how you connect your existing business phone number to Ring Jenny. Instead of replacing your number, you simply instruct your phone or carrier to redirect incoming calls to Ring Jenny's number. Your customers keep calling the same number they always have — Ring Jenny just picks up on the other end.

<Note>
  Your unique Ring Jenny number is shown in your **Dashboard** under **My Number**. You'll need it before completing any of the steps below.
</Note>

## Choose Your Forwarding Mode

There are two ways to forward calls to Ring Jenny, depending on how you want to manage incoming calls:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Always Forward" icon="phone-arrow-right">
    Every incoming call goes directly to Ring Jenny, regardless of whether your phone is available. Best for businesses that want Ring Jenny to handle all calls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Conditional Forward" icon="phone-missed">
    Calls only forward to Ring Jenny when your line is busy, unanswered, or unreachable. Best if you still want to answer calls yourself when available, with Ring Jenny as a backup.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  If you use **Conditional Forwarding**, make sure your "no answer" ring duration is set to **2–3 rings (10–15 seconds)** before the call forwards. If it's set too high, your carrier voicemail may intercept the call before Ring Jenny gets a chance to answer. Disable carrier voicemail for your business line if possible, or set the no-answer timer to the lowest available option.
</Warning>

## Forwarding Instructions by Device

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    **Always Forward (recommended for most businesses)**

    1. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone.
    2. Scroll down and tap **Phone**.
    3. Tap **Call Forwarding**.
    4. Toggle **Call Forwarding** on.
    5. Tap **Forward To** and enter your Ring Jenny number.
    6. Tap **Call Forwarding** in the top-left to go back and confirm the number is saved.

    Your iPhone screen will show a small phone-with-arrow icon in the status bar while call forwarding is active.

    **Conditional Forwarding (forward when busy or unanswered)**

    iPhone's native Settings menu only supports Always Forward. To set up conditional forwarding on an iPhone, contact your mobile carrier directly or use your carrier's account app to configure:

    - **Forward when busy**
    - **Forward when unanswered**
    - **Forward when unreachable**

    Enter your Ring Jenny number as the destination for each condition you want to enable.

    <Tip>
      Most carriers (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile) support conditional forwarding through their online account portals or by calling customer support.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Android">
    **Always Forward and Conditional Forward**

    The exact menu path varies slightly by Android manufacturer, but the steps below work for most devices:

    1. Open the **Phone** app.
    2. Tap the **three-dot menu** (⋮) or **More** in the top-right corner.
    3. Tap **Settings**.
    4. Tap **Calls** (some devices show this as **Calling accounts** or your carrier name).
    5. Tap **Call Forwarding**.
    6. Choose your forwarding type:
       - **Always forward** — enter your Ring Jenny number and tap **Enable**
       - **Forward when busy** — enter your Ring Jenny number and tap **Enable**
       - **Forward when unanswered** — enter your Ring Jenny number and tap **Enable**
       - **Forward when unreachable** — enter your Ring Jenny number and tap **Enable**

    <Tip>
      On Samsung Galaxy devices, the path is: **Phone app → ⋮ → Settings → Supplementary services → Call forwarding**.
    </Tip>

    <Note>
      If you don't see a **Call Forwarding** option in your Phone settings, your carrier may manage forwarding through their own app or website. Check your carrier's support documentation for model-specific instructions.
    </Note>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Landline / VoIP">
    Most landline and VoIP providers support call forwarding through standard dial codes. The most common codes are listed below, but check with your specific carrier to confirm they apply to your service.

    **Enable Always Forwarding**

    1. Pick up your handset and listen for a dial tone.
    2. Dial `*72` followed immediately by your Ring Jenny number (no spaces or dashes).
    3. Wait for a confirmation tone or message, then hang up.

    **Cancel Always Forwarding**

    1. Pick up your handset.
    2. Dial `*73`.
    3. Wait for a confirmation tone, then hang up.

    **Enable Conditional Forwarding (when no answer)**

    Some providers use `*92` to set a no-answer forward. Dial `*92` followed by your Ring Jenny number, then wait for confirmation.

    **Cancel Conditional Forwarding**

    Dial `*93` to cancel a no-answer forward on supporting carriers.

    <Warning>
      Dial codes vary by carrier and region. If `*72` or `*73` don't work, contact your phone provider to get the correct forwarding codes for your service. VoIP platforms like Google Voice, RingCentral, and Grasshopper manage call forwarding through their web dashboards rather than dial codes — log in to your account and look for a **Call Forwarding** or **Routing** setting.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Test Your Forwarding Setup

After enabling forwarding, always run a quick test before relying on Ring Jenny for real customer calls:

1. Call your business number from a different phone (a friend's phone or a second mobile line works well).
2. Confirm Ring Jenny answers within 2–3 rings with your custom greeting.
3. Check your Ring Jenny dashboard — the test call should appear under **Recent Calls** within a few seconds of hanging up.
4. Verify you receive an SMS and email notification with a call summary.

<Tip>
  If the call goes to voicemail instead of Ring Jenny, double-check that: (1) you entered the Ring Jenny number correctly in your forwarding settings, and (2) carrier voicemail is disabled or its no-answer timer is set lower than your forwarding timer.
</Tip>

## Disable or Change Forwarding

You can turn off call forwarding at any time using the same menu or dial codes used to enable it. Your Ring Jenny number and settings are preserved — simply re-enable forwarding whenever you want Ring Jenny to start answering calls again.
