## **📞 Forwarding Calls to Ring Jenny**

Ring Jenny is always ready to answer your calls—but when she’s on the clock is totally up to you. You control when Ring Jenny is active by turning **call forwarding** on or off for your business phone number. Forwarding your calls to your custom Ring Jenny number lets her **answer calls, respond to customers, and handle things on your behalf**—without changing your existing number. The Ring Jenny team will never interrupt your current service, and we’ll always notify you by email before you get close to any overage charges. Read through the guide below to understand how call forwarding works and how to set it up for your specific phone provider.

---

## **🔍 What Is Call Forwarding?**

Call forwarding is when your phone sends incoming calls to another number automatically—like your Ring Jenny number. Once it's set up, your original business number stays the same, but Ring Jenny handles the calls in the background.

---

## **🔁 Types of Call Forwarding**

There are two main types of call forwarding:

- **Unconditional Forwarding**\
  Forwards **all** calls to Ring Jenny —immediately and automatically.\
  ​
- **Conditional Forwarding**\
  Only forwards calls **in specific situations**:
  - **If busy** (when you're already on the phone)
  - **If no answer** (when you don’t pick up)
  - **If unreachable** (when your phone is off or has no signal)
  ⚠️ If you want Ring Jenny to handle **more than one condition** (like both _busy_ and _no answer_), you'll need to set each one up **separately** using its own code.
  Not all carriers support conditional forwarding.

---

## **⚙️ How to Set Up Call Forwarding**

Most carriers use special dial codes to set up forwarding and it will look like something similar to the steps outlined below:

1. Open your phone’s dialer (like you're making a call).
2. Enter the setup code **plus** your Ring Jenny number.\
   For example: `**21*1234567890#`
3. Press **Call**.
4. You’ll usually hear a confirmation tone or message.

⚠️ **IMPORTANT:** Setup codes vary by carrier.\
Be sure to scroll down and follow the specific instructions for your carrier below to ensure Ring Jenny works correctly.

---

## **📵 iPhone Users: Turn Off Live Voicemail**

If you’re using an iPhone, be sure to turn off **Live Voicemail**.\
This feature can prevent Ring Jenny from receiving forwarded calls when you don’t answer—especially if you're using conditional forwarding.

**To turn it off:**

1. Open the **Settings** app
2. Tap **Phone**
3. Tap **Live Voicemail**
4. Toggle it **off**

Ring Jenny works best when your iPhone isn’t trying to screen or answer calls on its own.

---

## **🔁 How to Turn Off Call Forwarding**

To turn off call forwarding, you'll either dial a special code from your phone or disable it in your provider’s app or settings.

- To disable **all forwarding**:
  - Dial your carrier's cancel code (like `##21#` or `*73`) and press Call.
- To disable **specific conditions** (for GSM networks like AT&T and T-Mobile):
  - `##67#` disables forwarding when **busy**
  - `##61#` disables forwarding when **no answer**
  - `##62#` disables forwarding when **unreachable**

🧠 **Heads Up:** Not all carriers support these specific disable codes.

- The codes above work for most GSM networks—like AT&T, T-Mobile, and providers like Cricket, Metro, and Mint.
- **Verizon and Boost Mobile** don’t support condition-specific disable codes. Use `*73` to turn off all forwarding instead.
- **Google Voice, RingCentral, and other VoIP services** require you to manage forwarding in their app or website.

When in doubt, check your carrier’s section below for exact instructions.

## **➤ Google Voice**

**Unconditional Forwarding (All Calls):**\
To forward calls from Google Voice to Ring Jenny, you’ll need to link your Ring Jenny number and verify it.

1. Log into your Ring Jenny and go to **Account \> Account Settings**
2. Enable the **"Call Forwarding Verification - SMS authentication code"** toggle

**You can setup and configure call forwarding from Google Voice desktop browser or from within Google Voice's device app.**

For Desktop -

1. In a new tab, go to [<u>Google Voice</u>](https://voice.google.com/)
2. In the top right, click the **Settings** gear icon
3. Under **Account \> Linked numbers**, click **New linked number**
4. Enter your Ring Jenny **number** and click **Send code**
5. Return to the Ring Jenny **\> Account Settings** tab and click **View text messages**
   - This will open a window displaying any verification codes sent to your Ring Jenny number
6. Back in Google Voice, enter the code received and click **Verify**
7. Once verified, go to **Settings \> Calls**
8. Under **Incoming calls**, **ensure Call Forwarding is enabled** for your linked Ring Jenny phone number.
9. Return to the Ring Jenny and disable verification code toggle.

For Device App -

1. Open Google Voice app on your device and go to settings (three bars hamburger menu in top left corner)
2. Tap into 'Devices and numbers'
3. Tap 'New linked number'
   1. Input your Ring Jenny phone number and **uncheck the 'number belongs to this device' option**
   2. click **Send code**
4. Return to the Ring Jenny **\> Account Settings** tab and click **View text messages**
   - This will open a window displaying any verification codes sent to your Ring Jenny number
   - Remember to disable verification code toggle.
5. Back in Google Voice App, enter the code received and tap **Verify**
   1. Google voice automatically enables call forwarding when you link a number, however it's best to double check
6. In the Google Voice app, tap back out of Devices and numbers.
7. Still within settings, tap into 'Incoming Calls'
   1. Ensure call forwarding toggle is enabled for your Ring Jenny phone number.

**Conditional Forwarding:**\
Not supported. Google Voice only allows full (unconditional) call forwarding.

**To Disable Forwarding:**

- Toggle it off under **Settings \>** **Calls \> Incoming calls**
- Or go to **Settings \> Linked numbers** and remove the Ring Jenny number

**Notes:**

- You must verify your Ring Jenny number before Google Voice will forward to it
- Setup must be completed via web browser, not the mobile app
- Call forwarding options vary depending on if you have the paid or free version.

## **➤ Zoom Phone**

📖 [<u>Full setup instructions from Zoom Phone support</u>](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0069180)

**Unconditional Forwarding (All Calls):**

1. Sign in to the [<u>Zoom web portal</u>](https://zoom.us/signin#/login)
2. Click **Phone** in the navigation menu
3. Go to the **Settings** tab
4. Under **Business Hours** or **Closed Hours**, click **Edit** next to **Call Handling**
5. Click **Add Phone Number** to forward to an external number (e.g., your Ring Jenny number)
6. Enter the country/region, phone number, and a description
7. Click **Save**

**Conditional Forwarding:**\
Zoom Phone supports forwarding based on:

- Business Hours
- Closed Hours
- Break Hours
- Holiday Hours

You can set different call handling rules for each time period by editing them individually in your settings.\
​

**To Disable Forwarding:**

- Return to the **Call Handling** settings for the relevant time period
- Remove the external number or toggle off the forwarding option

**Notes:**

- If you're unable to add an external number, your Zoom admin may need to allow it
