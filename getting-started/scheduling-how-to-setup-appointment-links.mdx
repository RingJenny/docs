Your Ring Jenny can easily assist callers looking to schedule an appointment with your business by texting them a direct scheduling link during the call. To set up this capability for your business, follow the steps below:

1. Navigate to **Agent Settings**
2. Locate the **Appointments** section
3. There are two available options for booking,\
   → **Schedule Appointment on Call** \
   → **Text Appointment Link**
4. Toggle 'On' **\[** **Schedule Appointment on Call \]** and select service ( for which you want appointment to be booked )
5. Toggle 'On'\+ Text Appointment Link' button
6. Copy and paste the link to your online business scheduler in the modal. You can also optionally customize the text that will be sent to your callers.
   1. This link and text can be updated at any time
7. Click 'Save' button in modal

Note: If the Accept Appointments section is toggled 'On' but no link is saved, your Ring Jenny agent will not allow callers to request appointments and will direct them to leave a message instead.
