Learn more about the different settings below:

Block 1-800 numbers: This setting prevents any 1-800 numbers from connecting to your Ring Jenny issued phone number. The 1-800 caller will receive an automated message that they are blocked, and no Ring Jenny account minutes will be used

Smart Spam Detection: This setting allows your Ring Jenny agent to detect scam, spam, and sales calls by analyzing conversational patterns. If Ring Jenny detects that the caller is attempting to sell your business something or is not interested in your business services, Ring Jenny will politely end the conversation and hang up

These conversations will still appear in your inbox and be marked with a SPAM call type tag

Blocked Numbers List: This is a custom list defined by your business of numbers that you wanted blocked from accessing your Ring Jenny issued phone number. Any phone number on this list will receive an automated message that they are blocked upon calling, and no Ring Jenny account minutes will be used. You can always easily remove a number from this list by clicking the garbage can icon next to the number you would like to remove from the list.
