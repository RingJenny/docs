Every robocall and spam solicitation that reaches Ring Jenny uses your plan's minutes — even if no real conversation takes place. Spam filtering lets you automatically detect and end those calls before they cost you anything, so your minutes are reserved for real customers. You can set filtering to be as broad or as precise as you need.

## How Spam Filtering Works

Ring Jenny automatically analyzes each incoming call for common spam signals — robocall audio patterns, known spam number databases, and caller behavior indicators. When a call matches these signals, Ring Jenny ends it immediately without consuming your minutes. No transcript is generated, and no notification is sent for calls blocked this way.

<Info>
  Spam filtering is included in all Ring Jenny plans at no extra cost. There's nothing to unlock or upgrade — just configure the settings to match your preferences.
</Info>

## What You Can Configure

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Block Toll-Free Numbers" icon="ban">
    Toggle on to automatically end all calls from toll-free numbers (1-800, 1-888, 1-877, etc.). Useful if you rarely receive legitimate calls from these prefixes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Custom Blocklist" icon="list">
    Add specific phone numbers you never want to reach Ring Jenny. Calls from these numbers are ended immediately, every time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sales Call Screening" icon="shield">
    Ring Jenny asks suspected sales callers to identify themselves and their purpose before the call continues — without hard-blocking them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Allowlist" icon="circle-check">
    Add numbers that should always get through, even if they would otherwise be caught by your spam rules. Use this to whitelist trusted contacts, suppliers, and partners.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Configure Spam Filtering

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Dashboard > Settings > Spam Filtering">
    Log in to your Ring Jenny dashboard, click **Settings** in the left sidebar, then select **Spam Filtering** from the submenu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Automatic Spam Detection">
    Make sure the **Automatic Spam Detection** toggle is turned on. This is Ring Jenny's baseline protection — it blocks robocalls and known spam sources without any additional configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Toll-Free Number Blocking (Optional)">
    If you want to block all calls from toll-free numbers, turn on the **Block Toll-Free Numbers** toggle. If you have suppliers, partners, or vendors who call from 1-800 numbers, add those numbers to your allowlist before enabling this setting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Sales Call Screening (Optional)">
    Turn on **Screen Suspected Sales Calls** if you'd prefer to filter solicitations without hard-blocking them. When a call is flagged as a potential sales call, Ring Jenny will say something like: *"Before I connect you, can you tell me what this call is regarding?"* Callers who identify themselves as legitimate will be connected; callers who hang up were likely not worth your time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Numbers to Your Blocklist (Optional)">
    Under **Custom Blocklist**, click **Add Number**, enter the phone number you want to block, and click **Save**. You can add as many numbers as you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Numbers to Your Allowlist (Optional)">
    Under **Allowlist**, click **Add Number** and enter any numbers that should always get through regardless of your other filtering settings. This is the right place to add your suppliers, partners, or any contact whose number might otherwise be caught by your rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Your Settings">
    Click **Save** to apply all your changes. Spam filtering rules take effect immediately on the next incoming call.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Be careful enabling **Block Toll-Free Numbers** if your suppliers, vendors, or business partners regularly call you from 1-800 or 1-888 numbers. Add those numbers to your allowlist first — otherwise those calls will be blocked automatically without any notification.
</Warning>

## About Sales Call Screening

Sales call screening is a softer alternative to hard-blocking. When Ring Jenny detects a potential solicitation, it asks the caller to identify themselves before continuing:

> *"Before I connect you, can you tell me what this call is regarding?"*

Legitimate callers — a real vendor, a partner, or a customer calling from a business line — will answer and get through. Robocalls and automated dialers will typically hang up or fail to respond, ending the call without using your minutes. This approach lets you filter aggressively without risking blocking someone you actually want to hear from.

<Tip>
  If a legitimate caller tells you they've been blocked or couldn't get through, add their number to your **Allowlist** under **Dashboard > Settings > Spam Filtering**. Allowlisted numbers are never blocked, regardless of your other settings.
</Tip>

## Reviewing Blocked Calls

Ring Jenny logs all automatically blocked calls in **Dashboard > Call History** under the **Blocked** filter. Review this list periodically to make sure no legitimate callers are being caught. If you spot a real number, add it to your allowlist immediately.

<Note>
  Calls blocked by spam filtering do not count against your plan's monthly minutes. Only calls that Ring Jenny actively handles — greets, converses with, or takes a message from — use your minutes.
</Note>
